# 🎨 Guia de Prompts para Geração de Imagens com IA
### Ferramentas Recomendadas: Midjourney v6, DALL-E 3 (ChatGPT/Bing) e Canva Magic Media
**Direção de Arte:** Estilo visual limpo, lúdico, colorido e acessível para o Ensino Fundamental, misturado com elementos de investigação policial e saúde pública.

---

## 1. Estação 2: A Cena do Crime (Quintal com Criadouros / Jogo dos 7 Erros)

* **Objetivo:** Ilustração panorâmica de um quintal brasileiro ensolarado de desenho animado, repleto de pontos visíveis onde o mosquito da dengue pode depositar ovos.
* **Uso:** Imprimir em banner A3 ou A2 plastificado para as crianças marcarem com "X" em EVA ou canetinha.

### Prompt para DALL-E 3 / Bing Image Creator:
```text
A vibrant and colorful cartoon comic-book illustration of a typical sunny Brazilian suburban house backyard, viewed from a slightly elevated eye-level angle. The backyard features clear hidden hazards collecting standing rainwater: an old car tire leaning on a red brick wall with water inside, potted garden plants with water overflowing in plastic saucers, an open unstoppered glass bottle on the grass, a clogged roof gutter with accumulated leaves and stagnant water, a blue plastic water barrel without a lid, and a dog water bowl on the patio. Clean bold lines, vibrant saturated colors, cheerful Disney/Pixar comic style, bright daylight, ultra-detailed, playful atmosphere, designed for a children's find-the-errors educational game, wide landscape orientation.
```

### Prompt para Midjourney v6:
```text
/imagine prompt: colorful cartoon comic illustration of a sunny Brazilian suburban backyard, clear daytime lighting, showing various objects collecting standing rainwater such as an old rubber tire, overflowing potted plant saucers, an open glass bottle, an uncovered blue water tank, and a clogged gutter, playful Hergé ligne claire comic style, bold outlines, vibrant colors, educational spot-the-hazard game scene for kids, wide shot --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6.0 --q 2
```

### Prompt para Canva Magic Media:
```text
Vibrant cartoon illustration of a backyard with plants, old tire with water, uncovered water tank and flower pots, clean lines, colorful children book style, wide view.
```

Rich metallic gold borders, deep navy blue background within the medal, sharp clean vector icon style, isolated on a pure white background, symmetrical, professional graphic design for printing stickers and medals.

A crisp metallic paperclip holds a white index card on the side, with a stylized dark stencil silhouette of an Aedes aegypti mosquito with distinct white striped legs printed in the corner. Mysterious CSI detective investigation board background, cinematic studio lighting, sharp textures, high resolution.

## 💡 Dicas de Exportação para Gráfica:
1. **Upscale:** Após gerar as imagens no Midjourney ou DALL-E, use ferramentas gratuitas de aumento de resolução (como o *Upscayl* ou *BigJPG*) para dobrar o tamanho da imagem antes de mandar imprimir em banners A3/A2.
2. **Cores para Impressão:** Ao importar as imagens para o Canva ou Photoshop, salve o arquivo final em perfil **CMYK** para que as cores impressas fiquem tão vivas quanto na tela.
